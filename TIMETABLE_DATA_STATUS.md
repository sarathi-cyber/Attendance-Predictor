# Timetable data status

## Integrated
- All ten class labels from the supplied timetable PDFs are available in the section selector.
- The original PDF files are copied unchanged to `public/timetables/` and linked from the selected class.
- I ECE-A is labeled as a 2024–25 historical/reference timetable.
- The nine 2026–27 classes retain their source academic-year labels.

## Not yet enabled
The PDFs are scanned timetable images. Their timetable cells have not been fully transcribed and verified as structured subject/day/period records. To avoid fabricated schedules, the structured timetable arrays are empty and schedule-derived attendance predictions must not be treated as active. The existing editable attendance inputs are sample inputs, not extracted from these PDFs.

The semester planning calendar remains the project's existing 2026 date range. I ECE-A is historical and must not be used for 2026 predictions.
