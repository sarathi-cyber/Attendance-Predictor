# Attendance calculator

1. Open the app and select your class.
2. In **Add attendance**, enter the subject name, number of classes attended, and number conducted. The optional percentage field is not used for calculations; attended/conducted counts are authoritative.
3. Click **Add subject**. Overall attendance, subject percentages, and classes needed to reach 75% and 90% recalculate immediately.
4. Edit the Attended and Conducted values directly in the subject table. Remove a subject with the trash icon.

Formula: attendance = attended ÷ conducted × 100.

To reach a target T by attending every future class, the calculator uses the smallest whole number x satisfying (attended + x) / (conducted + x) ≥ T / 100.

This calculator uses the counts you enter. It does not infer attendance from the PDF timetable. The timetable PDFs are references until their grids are transcribed and verified. I ECE-A is historical (2024–25) and is not a current-semester prediction source.
